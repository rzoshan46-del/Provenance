1. Project Biography & Executive Summary

Provenance (CritX) was architected to address fundamental systemic failures in distributed scientific and industrial research. In conventional academic and industrial collaborations, intellectual property attribution is vulnerable to ghostwriting, research briefs leak through uncontrolled AI prompting, milestone payments suffer dispute-heavy delays, and student contributors frequently lose recognition for their core deliverables.

Designed for high-trust environments (such as hackFront India at IIIT Delhi), Provenance provides an immutable, transparent substrate where every charter acceptance, confidential brief disclosure, AI-assisted code commit, expert peer review, and rupee payout is cryptographically logged on an append-only hash chain.

2. The Core Problems Solved

Attribution & Plagiarism

Attribution Theft & Ghostwriting

Junior researchers and students regularly submit critical algorithms only to have credit claimed by institutional leads. Provenance cryptographically binds every submission to its author's identity and generates self-sovereign 64-hex credentials that no third party can revoke.

Confidentiality & AI Risk

AI Model Data Leaks & Prompt Injection

Sponsor research briefs contain proprietary formulations that must never be ingested by external multi-tenant AI models. The Provenance AI Firewall enforces role-scoped boundaries, strips prompt injection attacks, and attributes outputs strictly to human directors.

Financial Fairness

Arbitrary Splits & Escrow Disputes

Unclear milestone conditions lead to unpaid student labor and sponsor frustration. Funds are locked in smart escrow before work begins and released via the Hare-Niemeyer largest-remainder algorithm, strictly conserving every single rupee.

Information Leakage

Undetectable Document Leaks

When confidential research documents are downloaded, provenance is lost. Provenance embeds invisible, zero-width steganographic watermarks encoding reader identity and project hashes directly into exported research texts.

3. Architectural Invariants & Non-Negotiables

INV-01
Append-Only Hash Chain Ledger Every state-changing operation executes a call to ledger.append(). Modifying any prior record breaks SHA-256 linkage from that point onward, immediately detectable by independent verifiers.
INV-02
AI Agents Never Earn or Act Unattended All agentic AI output (scoping proposals, matching recommendations, code snippets) is generated in a pending state and must be explicitly approved by a designated human owner before landing.
INV-03
Explicit Conflict-of-Interest Filtering Domain experts associated with competing organizations (defined in conflict_orgs) are deterministically excluded from viewing confidential briefs or reviewing submissions.
INV-04
Zero Minor Exposure & Privacy Guarantees Minors under 18 receive zero public profile exposure (/u/{id} returns 404). Personal email addresses are never rendered on public endpoints.

4. End-to-End System Mechanics

STAGE 01
Project Posting
Sponsor posts problem summary, confidential brief, and initial escrow funding.
STAGE 02
Charter Definition
Sponsor and leads establish immutable charter rules, IP terms, and milestone requirements.
STAGE 03
Team Matching
AI engine evaluates candidate skills, conflict organizations, and verification credentials.
STAGE 04
Charter Acceptance
Contributors explicitly sign the charter version. Unsigned members cannot view confidential briefs.
STAGE 05
Milestone Escrow
Funds transition to funded status. Work cannot commence until escrow verification passes.
STAGE 06
Work & Attestation
Artifacts submitted with SHA-256 hashes, AI declarations, and automated similarity checks.
STAGE 07
Expert Review
Assigned domain expert reviews deliverables against acceptance criteria without conflict of interest.
STAGE 08
Release & Credential
Largest-remainder rupee split released to contributors; permanent cryptographic credentials minted.

5. Technical Stack & Data Architecture

Backend & Routing Core

  • Framework: FastAPI & Starlette ASGI framework
  • Templating: Jinja2 server-rendered architecture
  • Session Security: Cryptographic signed HTTP-only sessions
  • Authentication: Google OAuth 2.0 with dynamic redirect host negotiation and local persona emulation

Cryptographic Data Storage

  • Database: SQLite with 15 normalized relational tables
  • Hashing Standard: Canonical JSON serialization with SHA-256
  • Chain Structure: Hash linked list (entry_hash = H(prev_hash || payload))
  • Steganography: Invisible zero-width unicode watermark encoder
