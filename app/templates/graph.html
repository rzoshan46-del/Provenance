How the Graph is Modeled

CritX represents provenance as a directed graph across 7 core entity types:

User -AUTHORED-> Contribution
Connects verified contributors to submitted artifacts.
Contribution -FOR-> Milestone
Maps work delivery directly to contract deliverables.
Milestone -PART_OF-> Project
Hierarchical grouping of milestone objectives under living charters.
Review -REVIEWS-> Contribution
Domain expert evaluations, rubric scores, and peer decisions.
User -REVIEWED-> Review
Attribution of vetted industry experts performing evaluations.
Credential -CERTIFIES-> Contribution
Cryptographically verifiable skill badges and proof-of-contribution.
