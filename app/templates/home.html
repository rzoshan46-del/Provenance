{# ── 3 core principles ── #}
01 — Chain of custody

Tamper-evident by design

Every event — submission, review, payment, AI call — is SHA-256 hashed into an append-only chain. Modify one record and the chain breaks. No exceptions, no overrides.

02 — AI with brakes

Every AI output waits for a human

The scoping agent, matching engine, and workspace agent all produce pending results. A human must approve before anything changes. The firewall strips hostile instructions from source documents.

03 — Fair attribution

Credits you can verify

Contribution credits are computed directly from the on-chain ledger. They can't be self-assigned. Credentials are 64-hex hashes tied to your contribution hash — no institution can revoke them.
